## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone V3.3 transforms the inventory replenishment subsystem from static, manual threshold rules (`min_qty`, `max_qty`) into an **intelligent, data-driven forecasting and self-tuning safety stock engine**. By analyzing historical outbound consumption (sales shipments and inter-warehouse transfer distributions) and lead-time volatility, the platform dynamically predicts forward demand and calculates mathematically optimal safety stock levels.

Key capabilities delivered in Milestone V3.3:
1. **Pure-Python Time-Series Demand Forecasting Core**:
   - High-performance, zero-C-dependency algorithms:
     - **Holt's Linear Trend (Double Exponential Smoothing)** with trend damping ($\phi = 0.95$).
     - **Holt-Winters Additive Triple Exponential Smoothing** with seasonal periodicity.
     - **Croston's Method for Intermittent Demand** (decomposing lumpy demand into non-zero demand size and inter-arrival intervals).
     - **Single Exponential Smoothing (SES)** & **Simple Moving Average (SMA)**.
   - Out-of-sample backtest evaluation computing **MAE**, **RMSE**, and **MAPE**.
   - Automated model selection (`model="auto"`) choosing the optimal model based on error minimization and intermittent demand ratios ($>45\%$ zeroes).
   - Dynamic 95% confidence interval projection bands ($\text{forecast} \pm 1.96 \cdot \hat{\sigma}_{\text{residual}} \cdot \sqrt{1 + 0.05 \cdot h}$).
2. **King/Silver Dynamic Safety Stock & Reorder Point Optimizer**:
   - Accounts for both daily demand variance ($\sigma_D$) and supplier lead-time variance ($\sigma_L$):
     $$SS = Z \times \sqrt{L \times \sigma_D^2 + \bar{D}^2 \times \sigma_L^2}$$
     $$ROP = (\bar{D} \times L) + SS$$
     $$Max = ROP + \max(10, \bar{D} \times \text{review\_days})$$
   - Risk categorization comparing current policies against recommended values:
     - `UNDERSTOCK_RISK`: Stockout probability exceeds target threshold under demand swings.
     - `OVERSTOCK_CAPITAL_LOCKED`: Thresholds exceed statistical ROP by $>30\%$, tying up working capital.
     - `BALANCED`: Thresholds are well-aligned with statistical variance.
3. **Automated Policy Tuning & Management APIs**:
   - `GET /api/v1/inventory/forecasting/demand`: Projects historical demand and forward forecasts with confidence intervals.
   - `GET /api/v1/inventory/forecasting/policy-recommendations`: Audits active reorder policies and provides dynamic tuning recommendations.
   - `POST /api/v1/inventory/reorder-policies/{id}/apply-tuning`: One-click application of recommended thresholds to `WarehouseReorderPolicy` with audit tracking (`last_tuned_at`, `tuning_method`).
4. **Visiq AI Conversational Analyst Integration**:
   - Deterministic `forecast_demand` tool added to `VisiqTools`.
   - Natural language intent routing in `VisiqAgent` ("Forecast demand for Industrial Motor over next 30 days").
   - 3-trace reactive Plotly.js visualization rendering historical consumption actuals, a 95% shaded confidence interval ribbon, and the projected forecast trajectory.
5. **Database Migration (`0023_predictive_demand_tuning.py`)**:
   - Extended `warehouse_reorder_policies` with `service_level`, `safety_stock`, `last_tuned_at`, and `tuning_method`.

## 2. Architecture & Forecasting Lifecycle

```
[ Historical Demand Events ]
├── Posted Sales Shipments
└── Outbound Stock Transfers
           │
           ▼
[ Daily Time-Series Continuous Array ]
           │
           ▼
[ Model Evaluation & Selection (model="auto") ]
├── Croston's Method (if intermittent ratio >= 0.45)
├── Holt-Winters Triple ES (if seasonal history >= 2 cycles)
└── Holt's Linear Trend / SES (lowest RMSE backtest)
           │
           ▼
[ 95% Confidence Interval Projection ]
           │
           ▼
[ King/Silver Safety Stock Optimizer ]
├── Service Level Z-factor (90%, 95%, 98%, 99%)
├── Average Daily Demand (D) & Std Dev (sigma_D)
└── Average Lead Time (L) & Variance (sigma_L)
           │
           ├──► [ Visiq Conversational Analyst & Plotly Visualizer ]
           │
           └──► [ One-Click Reorder Policy Auto-Tuning ]
                ├── min_qty updated to statistical ROP
                ├── safety_stock snapshot updated
                └── last_tuned_at & tuning_method recorded
```
