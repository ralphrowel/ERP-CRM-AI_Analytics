## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone V2.1 begins **Phase 3 (Post-V2 Advanced Capabilities)** by establishing the core financial foundation for the ERP platform: a formal **Double-Entry General Ledger (GL)**. 

Prior to V2.1, the application tracked commercial transactions in operational subledgers (Sales Invoices, AR Payments, Supplier Bills, AP Disbursements) without formal journal entries or real-time balancing against a financial Chart of Accounts. V2.1 introduces a compliant, audit-ready General Ledger engine enforcing:

1. **Enterprise Chart of Accounts (COA)** tailored for Philippine standard business operations with parent-child hierarchies and reconcilable account tagging.
2. **Fiscal Periods** with strict validation preventing postings to closed or future periods.
3. **Double-Entry Journal Vouchers** with gapless sequential numbering (`JV-YYYY-000001`), database check constraints, and invariant balance validation (`SUM(debit) == SUM(credit)`).
4. **Permanent Immutability & Reversal Workflow (ADR-0016)** guaranteeing that posted journal entries cannot be edited or deleted, requiring counter-entry reversals.
5. **Real-Time Trial Balance Generation** with zero-discrepancy equilibrium validation and normal balance accounting.

## 2. Key Capabilities Delivered in V2.1

### 1. Database Schema & Alembic Migration (`0018_general_ledger_core.py`)
- `accounts`: Chart of Accounts master with `code`, `name`, `account_type` (`asset`, `liability`, `equity`, `revenue`, `expense`), `normal_balance` (`debit`, `credit`), `is_active`, `is_reconcilable`, and `parent_id` for tree structures.
- `fiscal_periods`: Accounting period boundaries with `start_date <= end_date` and `status` (`open`, `closed`).
- `journal_entries`: Journal vouchers with `entry_no`, `posting_date`, `status` (`draft`, `posted`, `reversed`), `total_debit`, `total_credit`, `reversal_of_id`, and `version` for optimistic locking.
- `journal_entry_lines`: Debit and credit posting legs with non-negative constraints (`debit_amount >= 0`, `credit_amount >= 0`), non-zero activity constraint (`debit > 0 OR credit > 0`), and unique `(journal_entry_id, line_no)`.

### 2. General Ledger Domain Service (`GLService`)
- **COA Seeding**: Out-of-the-box seeding of standard enterprise accounts (Cash, Bank Operating, AR, Inventory, Input VAT, AP, GRNI, Output VAT, Capital, Retained Earnings, Sales Revenue, Sales Discounts, COGS, Operating Expenses).
- **Fiscal Period Lifecycle**: Validates that transaction dates fall within open fiscal periods prior to posting.
- **Double-Entry Balancing Engine**: Validates centavo-accurate equilibrium across all posting legs before transition from `draft` to `posted`. Imbalanced vouchers raise `IMBALANCED_JOURNAL_ENTRY`.
- **Reversal Engine (ADR-0016)**: Clones the posted voucher, swaps debit and credit allocations, links `reversal_of_id`, updates the original voucher status to `reversed`, and posts the counter-entry atomically.
- **Trial Balance Reporting**: Calculates aggregated debits, credits, and net balances according to normal account conventions, verifying `is_balanced == True`.

### 3. REST API & RBAC Security (`app/modules/accounting/router.py`)
- `POST /api/v1/accounting/accounts` (requires `accounting:manage`)
- `GET /api/v1/accounting/accounts` (requires `accounting:read`)
- `POST /api/v1/accounting/fiscal-periods` (requires `accounting:manage`)
- `POST /api/v1/accounting/journal-entries` (requires `accounting:entry:create`)
- `POST /api/v1/accounting/journal-entries/{id}/post` (requires `accounting:entry:post`)
- `POST /api/v1/accounting/journal-entries/{id}/reverse` (requires `accounting:entry:reverse`)
- `GET /api/v1/accounting/journal-entries/{id}` (requires `accounting:read`)
- `GET /api/v1/accounting/reports/trial-balance` (requires `accounting:report:read`)
