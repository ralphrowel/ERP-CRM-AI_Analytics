## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone V2.5 delivers the crowning capstone of Phase 3: **Integrated Financial Intelligence (Capstone-V3)**. It unites the double-entry General Ledger engine (Milestone V2.1), automated subledger posting (Milestone V2.2), multi-currency FX transactions (Milestone V2.3), and dbt transformation marts (Milestone V2.4) into a unified, cross-reconciled financial intelligence platform.

### Core Objectives Delivered:
1. **End-to-End GL-to-BI Analytical Pipeline**:
   - Registered operational accounting tables (`accounts`, `fiscal_periods`, `journal_entries`, `journal_entry_lines`, `currencies`, `fx_rates`) into the extraction registry (`analytics/config.py`).
   - Seamlessly extracts operational records from `erp_crm` into `raw.*` and transforms them via dbt models into `dim_account` and `fact_gl_journal_line`.
2. **Automated Cross-System GL Reconciliation Engine (`analytics/reconciliation/`)**:
   - `GLReconciler` continuously audits mathematical and accounting invariants between the OLTP operational General Ledger (`erp_crm`) and the OLAP analytical mart (`erp_analytics.mart`).
   - Verifies operational double-entry balance, analytical mart balance, cross-system total debit/credit parity, and per-account balance parity with exact centavo precision.
3. **Power BI / DAX Financial Ratio Semantic Model (`analytics/bi/`)**:
   - Expanded the Power BI Tabular Semantic Model with `dim_account`, `fact_gl_journal_line`, and conformed date/account star schema relationships.
   - Authored formal DAX measures for enterprise financial ratios:
     - `KPI-020 Current Ratio`: Short-term liquidity coverage (`Current Assets / Current Liabilities`).
     - `KPI-021 Quick Ratio`: Acid-test liquidity excluding merchandise inventory.
     - `KPI-022 Working Capital`: Net operational liquidity buffer (`Current Assets - Current Liabilities`).
     - `KPI-023 Operating Margin %`: Operating profitability margin before financing.
     - `KPI-024 Net Profit Margin %`: Bottom-line corporate profitability percentage.
     - `KPI-025 Debt to Equity`: Long-term financial leverage and solvency ratio.
     - `KPI-026 Return on Equity (ROE)`: Capital efficiency and equity return rate.
     - `KPI-027 GL Balance Integrity`: Absolute variance check guaranteeing balanced ledger.
4. **Visiq AI Conversational Financial Analyst (`analytics/visiq/`)**:
   - Integrated new deterministic analytical tools:
     - `financial_statement_summary`: Queries and evaluates formal Balance Sheets and Income Statements directly from analytical marts.
     - `gl_reconciliation_audit`: Executes instant cross-system reconciliation audits.
   - Upgraded Visiq conversational intent routing, KPI aliases (`current ratio`, `working capital`, `roe`, `balance sheet`, `income statement`, `reconciliation`), and guardrailed narrative synthesis.
   - Registered curated `mart_financial` dataset with executive starter questions.
