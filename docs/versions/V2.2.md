## 1. Executive Summary

Milestone V2.2 bridges the operational modules of the ERP platform (Sales & Purchasing) directly to the core Double-Entry General Ledger established in Milestone V2.1. 

Prior to V2.2, General Ledger entries required manual entry or independent creation. With V2.2, all commercial lifecycle events across operational subledgers now execute **real-time, automated General Ledger journal voucher postings**, ensuring that the financial books reflect operational reality instantly with zero reconciliation lag.

Furthermore, Milestone V2.2 delivers comprehensive, real-time financial reporting engines:
1. **Balance Sheet Engine** (`GET /api/v1/accounting/reports/balance-sheet`) providing point-in-time financial position across Assets, Liabilities, and Equity, mathematically balanced against current period retained earnings (`Assets == Liabilities + Equity + Net Income`).
2. **Income Statement (P&L) Engine** (`GET /api/v1/accounting/reports/income-statement`) detailing multi-step profitability including Gross Profit, Operating Income, and Net Profit across customizable date intervals.
3. **Automated Audit-Compliant Reversals**: Full adherence to ADR-0016; any voided subledger document (voided customer invoice, voided payment, voided supplier invoice, voided disbursement) automatically triggers an offsetting GL counter-entry reversal.

## 2. Key Capabilities Delivered in V2.2

### 1. Automated Subledger-to-GL Event Hooks
All subledger hooks automatically populate sequential `JV-YYYY-XXXXXX` vouchers with balanced debits and credits:

- **Sales Invoice Issuance (`SalesService.issue_invoice`)**:
  - `Dr. 1200 Accounts Receivable` (Grand Total)
  - `Cr. 4100 Sales Revenue` (Subtotal Net of Tax)
  - `Cr. 2210 Output VAT Payable` (Tax Total, if > ₱0.00)
- **Sales Invoice Voiding (`SalesService.void_invoice`)**:
  - Automatically identifies posted `invoice` vouchers and executes an atomic counter-entry reversal, updating original voucher status to `reversed`.
- **Customer Payment Allocation (`SalesService._execute_allocation`)**:
  - `Dr. 1120 Cash in Bank - Operating` (Allocated Amount)
  - `Cr. 1200 Accounts Receivable` (Allocated Amount)
- **Customer Payment Voiding (`SalesService.void_payment`)**:
  - Automatically reverses associated `payment` GL vouchers.
- **Credit Note Issuance (`SalesService.create_credit_note`)**:
  - `Dr. 4200 Sales Discounts & Allowances` (Subtotal)
  - `Dr. 2210 Output VAT Payable` (Tax Reversal, if > ₱0.00)
  - `Cr. 1200 Accounts Receivable` (Grand Total)
- **Supplier Bill Approval (`PurchasingService.approve_supplier_invoice`)**:
  - `Dr. 2150 Goods Received Not Invoiced (GRNI)` (Subtotal Net of Tax)
  - `Dr. 1400 Input VAT` (Tax Total, if > ₱0.00)
  - `Cr. 2100 Accounts Payable` (Grand Total)
- **Supplier Bill Voiding (`PurchasingService.void_supplier_invoice`)**:
  - Reverses associated `supplier_invoice` GL vouchers.
- **Supplier Disbursement (`PurchasingService.create_supplier_payment`)**:
  - `Dr. 2100 Accounts Payable` (Allocated Amount)
  - `Cr. 1120 Cash in Bank - Operating` (Allocated Amount)
- **Supplier Disbursement Voiding (`PurchasingService.void_supplier_payment`)**:
  - Reverses associated `supplier_payment` GL vouchers.

### 2. Financial Statements Reporting Engine (`GLService`)
- **Balance Sheet Report (`generate_balance_sheet`)**:
  - Aggregates all posted journal entry lines up to the specified `as_of_date`.
  - Computes sub-balances for Assets (`normal_balance='debit'`), Liabilities (`normal_balance='credit'`), and Base Equity (`normal_balance='credit'`).
  - Seamlessly computes Current Period Net Income from all revenue and expense accounts and folds it into total equity.
  - Verifies exact centavo mathematical equilibrium: `is_balanced = (total_assets == total_liabilities + total_equity)`.
- **Income Statement Report (`generate_income_statement`)**:
  - Summarizes revenues and expenses within the date interval `[start_date, end_date]`.
  - Calculates Revenues, Direct Costs (Cost of Goods Sold), Gross Profit, Operating Expenses, and Net Income.

### 3. REST API & Authorization Endpoints (`app/modules/accounting/router.py`)
- `GET /api/v1/accounting/reports/balance-sheet?as_of_date=YYYY-MM-DD`
  - Guarded by permission `accounting:report:read`.
- `GET /api/v1/accounting/reports/income-statement?start_date=YYYY-MM-DD&end_date=YYYY-MM-DD`
  - Guarded by permission `accounting:report:read`.
